# Reader 0.5.0

- **Table of contents.** The list button in a book now opens its contents first: chapters nested under their parts, the one you're reading marked, and a tap to go there. For converted books whose own contents list is empty or nearly so, Reader builds one from the chapters' titles. PDFs show their outline with page numbers.
- **Search inside a book.** Tap the magnifier (⌘F or Ctrl+F on a computer) to find a word or phrase anywhere in the book; case and accents don't matter. Each match is listed with the text around it and its chapter or page. Open one to see it marked on the page, then step through the rest with the arrows at the bottom.
- **Edit a book's details.** Fix a wrong title or author, or pick any image as its cover, from the book's menu. Changes sync, and importing the same file again keeps them.
- **Volume buttons turn pages on Android.** Volume down moves forward and up moves back, gliding about two-thirds of a screen in the scroll layout so the last lines stay in view.
- **The screen stays on while reading on Android**, until the book goes untouched for 10 minutes. Both can be switched off in the display settings, under While reading.
- PDFs in the scroll layout now reach their last page, so they can show 100% and be marked finished.

Chosen covers sync to devices on 0.5.0. Devices on 0.1.4 or later keep syncing everything else, and pick them up once they update.

## Install

**Windows**: download `Reader-0.5.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` and run it. Installed copies update themselves.

**macOS**: Reader updates itself. Accept the prompt in the library. For a new install, open `Reader-0.5.0-macos-universal.dmg` and drag Reader to Applications. The first time, right-click Reader and choose **Open**.

**Android**: Reader offers the update in the library. Tap **Install** and confirm in Android's installer. For a new install, download `Reader-0.5.0-android-arm64.apk` on the phone and open it.
