# Reader 0.1.5

- **Tap anywhere on a highlight to edit it.** Taps between the lines of a highlighted passage now open its colours, copy and remove, instead of hiding the controls.
- **Android's back button closes panels.** With the notes panel, display settings or any other panel open, back now closes the panel instead of leaving the book.

## Install

**macOS**: Reader updates itself. Accept the prompt in the library. For a new install, open `Reader-0.1.5-macos-universal.dmg` and drag Reader to Applications. The first time, right-click Reader and choose **Open**.

**Android**: Reader offers the update in the library. Tap **Install** and confirm in Android's installer. For a new install, download `Reader-0.1.5-android-arm64.apk` on the phone and open it.
