# Reader 0.1.4

- **Highlights in five colours.** Select text in an EPUB or a PDF (long-press on a phone) and pick a colour. Tap a highlight to recolour, copy or delete it. Highlights sync between your devices. Scanned PDFs, which are pictures of pages without text, can't be highlighted.
- **Notes panel.** The list icon in a book now shows its highlights and bookmarks, with a colour filter.
- **All your highlights in one place.** The highlighter button in the library lists every book's highlights, with search and a colour filter. Tap one to open the book right there.
- **Reopening a book no longer jumps ahead on Android.** Reopening near the start of a chapter used to land about a chapter forward.

Update every device: earlier versions can't sync once a highlight exists, and show a sync error until they're updated.

## Install

**macOS**: Reader updates itself. Accept the prompt in the library. For a new install, open `Reader-0.1.4-macos-universal.dmg` and drag Reader to Applications. The first time, right-click Reader and choose **Open**.

**Android**: Reader offers the update in the library. Tap **Install** and confirm in Android's installer. On 0.1.2, download `Reader-0.1.4-android-arm64.apk` on the phone once and open it. For a new install, download the same APK on the phone and open it.
