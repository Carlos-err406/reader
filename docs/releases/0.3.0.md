# Reader 0.3.0

- **Collections.** Group books however you like (a series, a course, books to lend), and put a book in as many collections as you want. Create them from the library, add books from a book's menu, and rename or delete them; deleting a collection keeps its books. Collections sync between your devices.
- **Select several books at once.** Long-press a book on the phone (keep your finger down and drag to sweep over more, as in a photo gallery), or use **Select** on the Mac. Then add them all to a collection, favorite them or remove them together.
- **A tidier phone library.** Tap the library's title for one sheet with your sections, collections, highlights, sorting and settings. Search is behind its own button.
- **Book actions on the phone** open as a sheet with large buttons. On the Mac, right-click any book for its actions.
- **The continue-reading card opens the book** when you tap it; there's no Resume button any more.
- Fixes: tapping outside an open menu only closes it, the keyboard no longer covers a sheet's text field on Android, and closing one panel no longer closes the one under it.

Collections sync to devices on 0.3.0. Devices on 0.1.4 or later keep syncing everything else.

## Install

**macOS**: Reader updates itself. Accept the prompt in the library. For a new install, open `Reader-0.3.0-macos-universal.dmg` and drag Reader to Applications. The first time, right-click Reader and choose **Open**.

**Android**: Reader offers the update in the library. Tap **Install** and confirm in Android's installer. For a new install, download `Reader-0.3.0-android-arm64.apk` on the phone and open it.
