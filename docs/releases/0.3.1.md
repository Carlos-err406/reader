# Reader 0.3.1

- **Collections reach every device.** Collections made on one device while another was on an older version now sync once that device updates; before, it kept skipping them.
- **A tidier Mac library.** The sync status and About moved from below the books to the bottom of the sidebar.

## Install

**macOS**: Reader updates itself. Accept the prompt in the library. For a new install, open `Reader-0.3.1-macos-universal.dmg` and drag Reader to Applications. The first time, right-click Reader and choose **Open**.

**Android**: Reader offers the update in the library. Tap **Install** and confirm in Android's installer. For a new install, download `Reader-0.3.1-android-arm64.apk` on the phone and open it.
