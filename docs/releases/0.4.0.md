# Reader 0.4.0

- **Reader for Windows.** Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) get their own installer. It installs for your user without administrator rights, signs in to Google Drive like the Mac version, syncs with your other devices and updates itself.

Reader isn't code-signed on Windows yet, so the first time you run the installer Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Install

**Windows**: download `Reader-0.4.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` and run it.

**macOS**: Reader updates itself. Accept the prompt in the library. For a new install, open `Reader-0.4.0-macos-universal.dmg` and drag Reader to Applications. The first time, right-click Reader and choose **Open**.

**Android**: Reader offers the update in the library. Tap **Install** and confirm in Android's installer. For a new install, download `Reader-0.4.0-android-arm64.apk` on the phone and open it.
