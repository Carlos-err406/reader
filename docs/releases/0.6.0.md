# Reader 0.6.0

- **Open books from other apps.** On Android, tap a PDF or EPUB in your file manager or Downloads, or share it to Reader: it's added to your library and opened. On the Mac, use **Open With → Reader** or double-click an EPUB. On Windows, double-click an EPUB or use **Open with**. Reader doesn't take over PDFs from your usual viewer.
- **Adding books on Android works.** Picking a book with **Add book** on the phone failed with "Expected the book's bytes"; it imports now. Covers drawn on the phone are now kept instead of being redrawn every time.

## Install

**Windows**: download `Reader-0.6.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` and run it. Installed copies update themselves. To open EPUBs with Reader by double-clicking, install this version.

**macOS**: Reader updates itself. Accept the prompt in the library. For a new install, open `Reader-0.6.0-macos-universal.dmg` and drag Reader to Applications. The first time, right-click Reader and choose **Open**.

**Android**: Reader offers the update in the library. Tap **Install** and confirm in Android's installer. For a new install, download `Reader-0.6.0-android-arm64.apk` on the phone and open it.
