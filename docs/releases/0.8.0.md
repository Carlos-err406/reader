# Reader 0.8.0

- **Read aloud.** The headphones in the reader read the book to you from the top of the page, with your device's own voices. The sentence being read is marked, and the pages turn to follow it. The bar that appears pauses, changes the speed (0.75× to 2×) and picks a voice, which is remembered for each language. How the voices sound, and which languages you have, depends on the device; on Android, **Get more voices** in the voice list opens the phone's screen for adding languages.
- **Time read.** The reader's footer shows how long you've spent in the book, like "1 h 20 min read", added up across your devices.
- **Only reading time counts.** The time read and the reading speed behind the time left now count only while Reader is in front, not while it sits behind another window, in the background or with the screen off. Listening doesn't change your reading speed.
- **Tags.** Collections are now tags, and the library can filter by several at once: pick Finance and For Dummies to see only the books with both, within the section you're in (Reading, Finished…). **Clear** drops the filter. Devices still on an older version keep syncing them as collections.
- **Tools at the bottom.** The reader's actions sit in a toolbar at the bottom, in reach of a thumb: contents, search, read aloud, **Aa**, and **⋯** for bookmarking the page, favorites and, on desktop, full screen. The header keeps the book's title, now shown in full. A PDF's zoom is in **Aa**.

## Install

**Windows**: download `Reader-0.8.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` and run it. Installed copies update themselves.

**macOS**: Reader updates itself. Accept the prompt in the library. For a new install, open `Reader-0.8.0-macos-universal.dmg` and drag Reader to Applications. The first time, right-click Reader and choose **Open**.

**Android**: Reader offers the update in the library. Tap **Install** and confirm in Android's installer. For a new install, download `Reader-0.8.0-android-arm64.apk` on the phone and open it.
