# Reader 0.2.0

- **Library sections.** All books, Reading, Favorites, Finished and Not started, in a sidebar on the Mac and as chips on the phone, each with a count.
- **Continue reading.** The last unfinished book sits at the top of the library with its progress and a Resume button.
- **Search and sort.** Search the library by title or author (accents don't matter). Sort by recently read, title, author, date added or progress.
- **Favorites.** Star a book from its ⋯ menu or from the star in the reader. Favorites sync between your devices.
- **Finished books.** Reading to the end marks a book finished. You can also mark a book finished or not finished from its ⋯ menu.
- **Drag and drop on the Mac.** Drop PDFs and EPUBs from Finder onto the window to add them.
- **Sync moved to the bottom** of the library, next to About.
- **Panels on the phone** close when you swipe them down, and pulling down over an open panel no longer starts a sync.

Favorites and finished books sync to devices on 0.2.0. Devices on 0.1.4 or 0.1.5 keep syncing everything else.

## Install

**macOS**: Reader updates itself. Accept the prompt in the library. For a new install, open `Reader-0.2.0-macos-universal.dmg` and drag Reader to Applications. The first time, right-click Reader and choose **Open**.

**Android**: Reader offers the update in the library. Tap **Install** and confirm in Android's installer. For a new install, download `Reader-0.2.0-android-arm64.apk` on the phone and open it.
