# Reader 0.7.0

- **Time left.** The reader's footer shows how long the chapter and the book will take you, like "12 min left in chapter · 4 h in book". It learns your reading speed as you read, and the speed syncs, so reading on any of your devices counts.
- **Look at a highlight without losing your place.** Opening a highlight, bookmark or search result no longer moves your reading position. **Back to 37%** returns you to where you were, and **Stay here** keeps the new spot. Reading on for a few pages from there makes it your place too. Choosing a chapter in the contents still takes you there.
- **Highlights land where they are.** Opening a highlight or search result shows it on screen, instead of a page early or far down. In scroll layout it sits clear of the header.
- **Highlights next to images work.** Text that starts right after an icon or image can be highlighted again, and highlights saved that way earlier now show up.
- **Highlighting over a highlight merges them** into one, in the new colour, instead of stacking a second one on top. Removing a highlight removes any stacked under it, so it disappears the first time.
- **Small print in boxes is readable.** Books that shrink whole passages, like the sidebars of "For Dummies" books, are raised close to the size of the rest of the text, and their shaded boxes keep a tint in every theme.
- **Mark books finished in bulk.** Select books in the library and mark them finished, or not finished, all at once.
- The reader's footer no longer shows an EPUB's percentage twice.

## Install

**Windows**: download `Reader-0.7.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` and run it. Installed copies update themselves.

**macOS**: Reader updates itself. Accept the prompt in the library. For a new install, open `Reader-0.7.0-macos-universal.dmg` and drag Reader to Applications. The first time, right-click Reader and choose **Open**.

**Android**: Reader offers the update in the library. Tap **Install** and confirm in Android's installer. For a new install, download `Reader-0.7.0-android-arm64.apk` on the phone and open it.
